Gestures
  • Move your index finger to aim, pinch (thumb + index) to press.
  • Swipe an open hand: right = next, left = previous, up/down = volume.
  • Hold open palm = play/pause · hold fist = mute.
  • Voice: hold the mic button and speak, or turn on hands-free and say "Jarvis, next track".
  • Cursor mode: your finger moves the other computer's mouse, pinch clicks, two fingers scroll, hold a fist to leave.
